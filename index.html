— How it started

A sketchbook,
a market stall, and a first canvas.

It started with characters. For fifteen years Steven has been drawing them — filling sketchbooks, sharing the process on social media, and selling his art at local markets.

Those markets and feeds became his proving ground: a place to test ideas, meet people face to face, and learn what makes someone stop and look twice. His audience grew one post and one conversation at a time.

Then came his first client. Brim Coffee asked Steven to create an original canvas painting for their branding — hand-drawn florals, coffee beans and lettering that gave the brand a look entirely its own.

Today Steven brings that same hand-made character to every brand he works with — illustration, identity and artwork that extends a brand rather than just repeating it, delivered as flexible files that work wherever you take them.

— The Process

From idea to identity.

  1. i.

    Discovery

    A conversation about your brand, your audience and where the work will live — a launch, a campaign, a storefront, a screen.

  2. ii.

    Direction

    Moodboards, palettes and rough concepts, so every piece of the work feels like one considered family.

  3. iii.

    Design

    Refined artwork shown in context, revised together until it's unmistakably yours.

  4. iv.

    Delivery

    Layered source files, vector exports, color specs and usage guides — ready for any format, platform or partner you choose.
